# 🤝 Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this guide accurate for Kenyan businesses and developers!

- 🐛 **Found an error or outdated step?** Open an issue with a link or screenshot.
- ✍️ **Want to improve a section?** Fork, edit the Markdown, and open a pull request.
- 🌍 **Translations** (Kiswahili especially) are very welcome under `docs/sw/`.

Please keep contributions practical, vendor-neutral where possible, and free of affiliate links.
